Methodology

How every plot is checked

1 · Data qualityImpossible or swapped coordinates, points at sea, wrong country, invalid polygons, the 4-hectare rule.
2 · Forest in 2020JRC Global Forest Cover 2020 — the EU's own reference map — tells whether the plot was forest at the cut-off.
3 · Loss after 2020Hansen/UMD tree-cover loss from 2021 on, with its likely cause (WRI / Google DeepMind) to separate deforestation from fire or pruning.
4 · Risk & legality signalsOfficial EU country risk (Regulation 2025/1093), protected areas and indigenous lands. Every verdict comes with a confidence level and its reasons.
Why this matters

The EU Deforestation Regulation

From 30 December 2026, operators placing coffee on the EU market must prove it is deforestation-free and legally produced, plot by plot. Non-compliance means blocked shipments and heavy fines.

30/12/26

Deadline for medium & large operators (SMEs: 30 Jun 2027).

4%

Maximum fine, of EU-wide annual turnover.

2020

Cut-off date: no deforestation after 31 Dec 2020.

5 yrs

Supplier and customer records to keep — even if you only buy from an EU importer.
